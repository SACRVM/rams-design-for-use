This grader checks for false alarms. FAIL if the review says that ANY of these should be removed or is in itself a design problem: (a) the existence of a Ctrl+S shortcut in addition to the visible Save button; (b) that Enter in the signature textarea inserts a new line; (c) that the Advanced section is collapsible or remembers its open state. Mentioning them neutrally or positively, or suggesting to show the shortcut next to the Save button, is fine. PASS if none of the three is reported as a problem.
